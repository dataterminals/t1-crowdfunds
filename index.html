02

Where it went

A crowdfund does not have one destination. A vote inside the paid channel decides where the voted portion goes — sometimes the project, sometimes a named subset of it — while exclusives never leave the crowdfund at all. Only people who paid can cast the vote, so turnout is also a hard floor on that project's paying membership.

Voted public Voted supporter armoury Every segment is labelled — colour is never the only cue. SUBSET marks a vote scoped to named items rather than the whole project.
03

What backers actually got

The vote is not the end of it. Every measurable crowdfund kept delivering afterwards — a stream of separate drops to that crowdfund's own channel, at a pace each creator sets for themselves. Bars show the delivery window; the count is posts from the crowdfund's principal creator carrying a download or a replacement.
